Pictou County is a cluster of towns on the Northumberland Shore — New Glasgow, Stellarton, Westville, Trenton, and the Town of Pictou — plus rural roads, harbour lots, and the ferry corridor toward Prince Edward Island at Caribou. It is not a suburb of Halifax, and it is not the same market as [Truro and Colchester](/blog/truro-colchester-real-estate), even though both sit on Highway 104. Truro is the 102/104 hub facing Halifax. Pictou County faces the strait, with its own industrial history, hospital and service centre in the New Glasgow area, and housing stock that is often older than the furniture in the listing photos.

If you search “north of Halifax, cheaper,” you will mix these counties. Use the [map](/map). The pins are an hour or more apart, and the jobs are not the same.

## The towns, not one label

**New Glasgow** is the largest of the towns: a downtown grid, residential streets of older houses, and the commercial services people mean when they say they need a pharmacy and a grocery store without a highway trip. Housing ranges from modest older singles to larger homes on the edges. Flood talk near rivers is worth a site visit after rain.

**Stellarton, Westville, and Trenton** are their own municipalities with their own tax bills and their own mix of worker housing, later infill, and leftover industrial geography. Drive the specific town. A tidy Westville bungalow and a New Glasgow two-storey should not share an average in your head.

**Pictou town** sits on the harbour with a different tourist-and-waterfront feel, a different wind, and a different inventory of older houses. Waterfront and near-harbour lots need extra insurance and inspection attention. The [waterfront property](/blog/waterfront-property-nova-scotia) checklist applies when the pin is on the shore.

**Rural Pictou County** is wells, septic, wood lots, and small communities along the 104 or toward the shore. Winter driving on untreated back roads is part of ownership. So is a longer trip to specialists and inspectors.

County versus town property tax is not a rounding error. Ask for the actual bill. [PVSC](https://www.pvsc.ca/) assessment feeds the municipal levy; it is not market value.

## Highway 104, the ferry, and the Halifax question

Highway 104 is the spine. You can reach Truro, then the 102 to Halifax, in a time that looks acceptable on a summer Saturday and feels long as a five-day commute. Be honest:

- If the job is in Halifax, you are buying a weekend-and-hybrid house or a very long drive, not a Bedford-style suburb.
- If the job is local — health care, education, trades, retail, remaining industry, remote work — the county can be a complete daily life.
- If PEI or the North Shore is the point (family, ferry, cottage-to-year-round), Pictou County is a different logic than “close to the airport.”

Time the drive to the places you will actually go: the hospital, the grocery, the school, YHZ if you fly for work. Do not use a downtown New Glasgow to downtown Halifax estimate as if it were a Bedford Highway hop.

[How to buy a house in Nova Scotia](/blog/how-to-buy-a-house-in-nova-scotia) is the same sequence here: pre-approval, viewing, conditions, lawyer. Rural and older-town files need more inspection, not less.

## Older buildings, oil, and industrial neighbours

A lot of Pictou County housing was built when the region’s industrial base was larger. That can mean solid bones, small rooms, old wiring, oil tanks, and a neighbourhood that still lives next to commercial or former industrial land. Walk further than the listing’s photo radius.

Check:

- **Heat.** Oil remains common. Tank age and location matter to insurers and lenders. Heat pumps appear as retrofits; ask what they supplement.
- **Windows, insulation, and roofs.** Coastal and strait weather is hard on envelopes. [Heating costs](/blog/heating-costs-nova-scotia) in a leaky house will dominate a cheap purchase price.
- **Water and sewer.** Town services in the urban cluster; wells and septic outside. Water tests during conditions are normal.
- **Foundation and moisture.** Older basements, clay, and poor grade show up in inspections constantly.
- **PID and lot lines.** Especially near the water, rail, or old company housing. [Look up the PID](/blog/nova-scotia-property-lookup-pid). [Property Online](https://www.propertyonline.ca/) is the provincial parcel system your lawyer will use.

If the house has a wood stove, plan a WETT inspection for insurance. If it is a [heritage](/blog/heritage-homes-nova-scotia) or simply very old building, budget for specialists, not only a general inspector.

## Work, services, and university-adjacent demand

New Glasgow and the nearby towns are a regional service centre for the eastern North Shore. That supports a year-round rental and resale market that is not purely recreational. It is still smaller than HRM. Houses can sit, or a well-priced house can go quickly because the inventory of “ready, in town, municipal services” is thin.

Do not buy a second unit as an investment thesis from a national headline. If you are considering a duplex, that is a separate underwriting and zoning problem — and not something to improvise from another province. For a primary home, focus on the street, the heat, and the commute you will live.

Provincial information sits on [novascotia.ca](https://novascotia.ca/). For licensed REALTORs, see the [Nova Scotia Real Estate Commission](https://nsrec.ns.ca/).

## Pictou County versus Colchester versus HRM

A simple comparison for out-of-area buyers:

- **HRM:** jobs density, higher prices, bridges and the Bedford Highway. Start with the Halifax guide if that is really the life you want.
- **Truro / Colchester:** closer Halifax commute option, hub-town services, 102 access. Read the Truro article before you treat “central Nova” as one search.
- **Pictou County:** Northumberland Shore, town cluster around New Glasgow, longer Halifax drive, older housing, harbour and ferry geography.

Open three pins on the [Acres map](/map) — one in each — and drive them in your head at 7 a.m. in February. That exercise saves more money than arguing about average prices.

## How to look at listings here

1. Decide whether you need to be in the New Glasgow town cluster for services, or whether a shore or rural lot is the point.
2. Zoom the map to the town name, not “Pictou County” as a blob.
3. Separate municipal-service houses from well-and-septic houses.
4. [Save favorites](/blog/how-to-browse-homes-with-acres) and compare tax bills and heat type.
5. Inspect with someone who does oil, wells, and old foundations in this region.

Deed transfer tax is municipal and varies by town and county. Confirm with the municipality and your lawyer. [Closing costs](/blog/closing-costs-nova-scotia) still apply.
