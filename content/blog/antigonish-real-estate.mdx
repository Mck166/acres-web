Antigonish is a small town on Highway 104 with a county around it, a regional hospital, and St. Francis Xavier University in the middle of daily traffic. That mix — town lots, rural acreage, student-season rhythm — is the housing market. It is not Halifax with a different name, and it is not [Pictou County](/blog/pictou-county-real-estate) even though both sit on the eastern mainland and share the 104. New Glasgow is an industrial-and-service town cluster on the Northumberland Shore. Antigonish is a university town with a county of farms, shore roads, and gravel driveways.

If you shop “northeastern Nova Scotia” as one saved search, you will mix a walking-distance-to-Main-Street house with a ten-acre lot toward the coast. Use the [map](/map).

## Town of Antigonish versus the county

**The town** is the compact part: older residential streets, some newer infill, a commercial core, and the university’s gravity. You can, on some streets, walk to shops in a way you cannot in a rural subdivision. Parking, one-way patterns, and student-season congestion are part of those streets. Houses near campus can mean rental history, extra vehicles, and a different January than a quiet crescent at the edge of town.

**Antigonish County** is wells, septic, longer plowing, and communities that use the town for groceries and the hospital. Shore-facing roads and inland farm lots are different products again. “Ten minutes to town” in July can be a school-bus-and-snow conversation in February.

Municipal tax bills differ between town and county. Ask for the actual bill. [PVSC](https://www.pvsc.ca/) assessment is the valuation input, not the asking price. Deed transfer tax is municipal; confirm the rate with the municipality and your lawyer. [Closing costs](/blog/closing-costs-nova-scotia) still apply.

Provincial context and programs: [novascotia.ca](https://novascotia.ca/). Licensed REALTORs: [Nova Scotia Real Estate Commission](https://nsrec.ns.ca/).

## University life, without treating students as a business plan

St. Francis Xavier shapes housing whether you want it to or not. Academic-year traffic, rental demand near campus, and summers that feel quieter are real. That does not mean every buyer should underwrite a student rental.

If you want a quiet owner-occupied house, look at the specific street at move-in weekend and in February, not only in May. Ask how the property has been used. A legal suite and an informal student house are different zoning, insurance, and neighbour realities.

If you are considering a duplex or a house with extra kitchens because of the university, stop and read the [duplexes and small investment properties](/blog/duplexes-investment-properties-nova-scotia) caveats. Zoning, financing, fire separation, and tenancy rules are not a side hustle you invent on the drive from the 104. This article is not telling you to buy a rental.

## Highway 104, Pictou County, and Halifax

Antigonish sits further east than Truro and Pictou on the way toward Cape Breton. A Halifax commute as a daily habit is a long 104-and-102 life. Be honest:

- Local work (university, health care, schools, trades, retail, remote) makes the town a complete centre
- Hybrid work with flights out of YHZ is a calendar of highway hours
- A five-day downtown Halifax job is generally the wrong reason to buy here

Compare with Pictou County if your family is choosing among eastern mainland towns. Pictou County’s New Glasgow cluster is larger as a service centre in some ways and different in harbour-and-industry geography. Antigonish’s identity is the university and a smaller downtown. Drive both. Do not pick from average-price charts.

[How to buy a house in Nova Scotia](/blog/how-to-buy-a-house-in-nova-scotia) does not change in Antigonish: pre-approval, conditions, inspection, lawyer. What changes is comparable sales and how long a specialist takes to reach a rural septic.

## What the housing stock actually is

In town you will see older singles, some split-entries, infill, and a limited number of newer houses and townhouse-style buildings. In the county you will see farmhouses, bungalows on acreage, and coastal places that need the same caution as other shore property.

Inspection priorities match the rest of rural Nova Scotia:

- **Heat.** Oil, electric, heat pump, wood. [Heating costs](/blog/heating-costs-nova-scotia) in a tall older house will dominate a cheap sticker price. WETT if there is a wood stove.
- **Water and sewer.** Town services versus well and septic a few minutes out. Water tests and septic inspections belong in conditions; see the [home inspection checklist](/blog/home-inspections-nova-scotia).
- **Oil tanks.** Age and location; lenders and insurers care.
- **Roofs and moisture.** Wind off the uplands and the shore is hard on envelopes.
- **PID and lot.** Rural acreage can include wetland, a right-of-way, or a panhandle. [Look up the PID](/blog/nova-scotia-property-lookup-pid). [Property Online](https://www.propertyonline.ca/) is the provincial parcel system.

Heritage or simply very old town houses need extra patience. The [heritage homes](/blog/heritage-homes-nova-scotia) notes apply when the plaster and the foundation are the story.

## Daily life: hospital, Main Street, and winter

Antigonish functions as a service town for a wide rural area. That is good if you want a hospital, a downtown, and a university’s public events without living in HRM. It is a constraint if you need specialist retail every week — some trips still go to New Glasgow, Halifax, or online.

Winter is highway driving, student-season parking, and rural power outages. A generator is a lifestyle appliance in the county if you are on a well, not an eccentricity.

If you are moving from another province, visit in term-time and in a storm if you can. A July campus tour is not the housing market.

## How to look without mixing town and shore

1. Decide whether you need to be in town for walking, the hospital, or the university, or whether a county lot is the point.
2. Open [listings](/properties) or zoom the [map](/map) to the Town of Antigonish versus the shore and inland roads.
3. Separate municipal-service houses from well-and-septic houses on your shortlist.
4. [Save favorites](/blog/how-to-browse-homes-with-acres) and compare tax bills, heat, and whether the street is student-heavy.
5. Inspect with someone who does oil, wells, and old foundations in eastern Nova Scotia.

If you also have Pictou County on the list, keep two favorite folders in your head. The 104 connects them. The daily life does not merge.
