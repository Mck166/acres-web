Acres is built for people who want a simple way to look at real listings and keep track of the ones that stand out. The website uses the same property database and the same login as the Acres mobile app, so anything you save in one place shows up in the other.

## Browse the listing grid

The home page loads current properties from the Acres feed. Each card shows the photo, price, bedroom and bathroom counts, and address. You can keep scrolling with **Load more** to see the next page of homes.

You do not need an account to browse. Sign in when you are ready to save a place you like.

## Create an account or log in

Use the same email and password you already use in the Acres app. New visitors can sign up from the Sign Up page with an email address and a password of at least six characters.

Once you are signed in, your session stays active in this browser until you log out.

## Save properties to Favorites

Tap the heart on a listing card to add it to your favorites. Open **Favorites** in the header to see every home you have saved, newest first.

Removing a favorite on the website updates the same list the mobile app reads, so your saved homes stay in sync.

## Why this page exists

Buying a home starts with looking. Acres keeps that first step uncluttered: real listings, a short list of favorites, and the same account wherever you open the app.
